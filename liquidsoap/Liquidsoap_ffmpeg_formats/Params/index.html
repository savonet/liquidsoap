Module Liquidsoap_ffmpeg_formats.Params

What a process knows of the parameters of the three FFmpeg content kinds.

Kinds.Make takes one implementation of S and registers the kinds over it. Two implementations exist: libav's, linked by a process that streams, and the stand-in of dummy/, linked by a process that only typechecks. Both accept exactly the same annotations. Kind is the parameters of one kind.
