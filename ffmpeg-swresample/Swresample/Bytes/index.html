Module Swresample.Bytes

Raw samples in bytes, channels interleaved in the order of the channel layout, each sample in the machine's native representation of the sample format. The format is given at creation and is a packed one. With stereo signed 16-bit samples, three samples per channel take 12 bytes: L0 R0 L1 R1 L2 R2.
